The LinkedIn Basics activity taught me that LinkedIn is mainly used for professional networking and finding career opportunities. I learned that your profile is important because it shows your education, work experience, and skills to other people. I also learned that it is more important to make useful connections than just having a lot of connections. I can use this in the future by connecting with people in careers that interest me and looking for internships and job opportunities. The Honeycomb Model activity taught me that different social media sites are useful for different reasons. The model includes identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. I learned that businesses do not have to use every social media platform in the same way. Some platforms may be better for sharing content, while others may be better for networking or building relationships. I can use this in the future by thinking about what a business is trying to accomplish before choosing which social media platforms to use. This could help me in the future if I am marketing a business and want to reach the right audience and communicate with customers more effectively. The Instagram activity taught me that Instagram can be used for more than just posting pictures and videos. It can also be used by businesses to promote products, connect with customers, and reach a larger audience. I learned that having good content and knowing your audience is important when using Instagram for marketing. I can apply this in the future by using Instagram to help promote a business or brand. Since Instagram is very visual, it is useful for showing products and getting people interested in a business.
